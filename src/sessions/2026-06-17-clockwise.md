  You've had conversations that felt like they were heading somewhere. Your
  prospective buyer seemed engaged. The fit seemed right and then... nothing. No
  clear reason, it just stopped.


  This is not a pricing problem or a product problem; it is almost always a process problem. The way most sales conversations are structured creates the very hesitation they were designed to overcome. Buyers arrive carrying Fear, Uncertainty and Doubt before you have said a single word. And most sales processes, built around internal logic rather than the buyer's experience, make all three worse.


  This workshop shows you what is actually happening on the buyer's side of your sales conversations, and what changes when you design your process around how buyers actually think and make decisions.
